Key Takeaway: The "Sack Race" Convergence

Prime Minister tenures have shrunk at a much faster rate than Premier League managerial tenures. While Premier League managerial tenures have gradually shortened (a drop of 63.6% in average tenure since the 1990s as club patience waned), Prime Minister tenures have collapsed even faster (a drop of 87.6% in average tenure starting since 2020 compared to the 1990s). In the 1990s, a Prime Minister typically lasted over 2.5× longer than a Premier League manager; by the 2020s, the gap narrowed dramatically as rapid political turnover mirrored football's hire-and-fire culture.
