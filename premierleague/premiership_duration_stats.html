Key Takeaway: Convergence Date & Parity Analysis

Prime Minister tenures are shortening at a much faster rate than Premier League managerial tenures. While Premier League managerial tenures have contracted by 63.6% since the 1990s (from an average of ~930 days down to ~338 days), Prime Minister tenures have experienced a steeper decline of 87.6% (tumbling from ~3,027 days down to ~376 days for tenures starting since 2020).

Projected Convergence Date: Based on the linear regression trendlines of both roles since 1992 (with PM tenures dropping by ~80 days/year compared to ~20 days/year for football managers), the two trendlines intersect at late 2023 / early 2024 (~2023.9). In practical terms, parity is no longer a future theoretical event—the rapid turnover of the 2020s has already brought prime ministerial shelf-life into parity with Premier League managerial job security.
