Key Takeaway

Prime Minister tenures have shortened far faster than Premier League managerial stints. Manager tenures contracted by 63.5% since the 1990s (~930 → ~338 days), while PM tenures fell by 87.5% (~3,027 → ~376 days for 2020s starts).

Projected convergence: Linear regression puts the intersection in late 2023 / early 2024 (~2024.0). The rapid turnover of the 2020s brought prime ministerial job security into parity with Premier League managerial shelf-life.
